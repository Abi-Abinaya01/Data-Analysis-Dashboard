# **Business Performance & Profitability Analysis**
## Project Overview
This project analyzes a retail company’s sales dataset to evaluate overall business performance and profitability across different regions and product categories. The aim is to identify profitable regions, detect loss-making products, and determine whether higher sales always lead to higher profit.
## Business Questions
The analysis focuses on answering the following key questions:
• Which regions generate the most profit?
• Which products or sub-categories cause losses?
• Does higher sales always result in higher profit?
## Tools Used
• Excel – Data cleaning and pivot table analysis
• Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation
## Data Preparation (Excel)
The dataset was first analyzed in Excel using Pivot Tables to summarize sales and profit performance.
Steps performed:
1.	Loaded the dataset into Excel
2.	Created Pivot Tables to analyze Sales and Profit by Region
3.	Investigated product-level profitability to identify loss-making items
## Regional Sales and Profit Analysis
A pivot table was created to analyze Sales and Profit by Region.
Region	Sales	Profit	Insight
West	₹7,25,458	₹1,08,418	Highest sales and profit
East	₹6,78,781	₹91,523	Second most profitable
Central	₹5,01,240	₹39,706	High sales but low profit
South	₹3,91,722	₹46,749	Lower sales but still profitable
Key Findings
• West region generates the highest profit and sales.
• East region also performs strongly with consistent profitability.
• South region is profitable but contributes less revenue.
• Central region shows relatively high sales but very low profit, indicating margin issues.
Conclusion
West and East regions are the main profit drivers, while Central and South require performance improvement strategies.
## Sales vs Profit Relationship
The analysis demonstrates that high sales do not always guarantee high profit.
Example Cases
Central Region
•	Sales: ₹5,01,240
•	Profit: ₹39,706
Although sales are high, profit margins are very low.
South Region
•	Sales: ₹3,91,722
•	Profit: ₹46,749
Moderate sales with limited profit growth.
West Region
•	High sales and high profit, representing the ideal performance scenario.
Key Insight
Profitability depends on cost management, discounts, and product mix, not just revenue.
## Product-Level Profitability Analysis
Further analysis was performed to identify loss-making products in the Central region.
Loss-Making Sub-Categories
Sub-Category	Sales	Profit
Furnishings	₹15,254	-₹3,906
Tables	₹39,155	-₹3,560
Appliances	₹23,582	-₹2,639
Bookcases	₹24,157	-₹1,998
Machines	₹26,797	-₹1,486
Binders	₹56,923	-₹1,044

Key Observation
Several products generate strong sales but still result in losses.
Examples:
• Tables produce ₹39,155 in sales but result in a loss of ₹3,560.
• Bookcases produce ₹24,157 in sales but also generate negative profit.
This indicates that the issue is not low demand but poor profit margins, likely caused by discounts or high operational costs.
## Profitable Products
Some products consistently generate strong profits:
• Copiers → ₹15,609 profit
• Phones → ₹12,323 profit
• Accessories → ₹7,252 profit
These products contribute significantly to overall business profitability.

## Key Business Insights
The analysis reveals several important insights:
1.	West and East regions are the primary profit drivers.
2.	Central region generates high revenue but suffers from low profit margins.
3.	Several products generate sales but still result in losses.
4.	Profitability depends on product mix and pricing strategies rather than sales volume alone.
## Business Recommendations
Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:
• Review pricing and discount policies for loss-making products.
• Investigate cost structures for products such as Tables and Appliances.
• Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-margin products like Copiers and Phones.
• Improve profitability in the Central region by optimizing product mix and pricing strategies.
## Project Outcome
This analysis demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses:
• Identify profitable and underperforming regions
• Detect loss-making products
• Improve pricing and sales strategies
• Optimize product portfolio for higher profitability
